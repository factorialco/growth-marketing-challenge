## Welcome to the Growth Marketing Challenge!

At Factorial, our Growth team isn't just building features; we are building **engines for acquisition**. We look for engineers who think like marketers, act like data scientists, and code with a focus on impact.

In this challenge, we want to see how you balance **speed to market** with **technical scalability**. We value candidates who don't just follow a spec, but challenge it to ensure we are running the most effective experiments possible.

#### The Prompt: The "Leaky Bucket" Problem

> Marcus is the owner of a growing B2B SaaS business. He is currently spending €5,000/month on ads that drive traffic to a basic landing page, but his **conversion rate from visitor to "Demo Requested" is under 1%.**
> 
> He has asked for your help to transform this "leaky bucket" into a high-converting acquisition machine.

### **Your Objective:**

This is a live coding challenge. During the session you will design and prototype a solution that focuses on **lead capture and experimentation**.

**Things to consider (and for us to discuss):**

- **The Lead Flow:** How do we structure a multi-step form or an "alternative" strategy (e.g., a lead magnet or calculator) to increase conversion?
- **The Experimentation Layer:** How would you build this so we can A/B test a hypothesis (e.g., different headlines or form lengths) with minimal friction?
- **Tracking & Attribution:** What data points do we need to capture to ensure we know exactly which experiment "won"?

### What to prepare

We want to spend our time together solving problems, not configuring environments. Factorial is AI First, so we expect you to use AI during the challenge (full agentic-mode included).

- **The Setup:** Please clone the project and have it ready to go, we will ask you to share your screen. You can explore the code as much as you want.
- **The Toolkit:** Bring your AI coding partners (Cursor, Claude, etc.). We want to see how you leverage these to move fast and skip the boilerplate.
- **The Mindset:** Be ready to challenge Marcus (your interviewer). If his idea seems slow to implement or unlikely to convert, tell him why and suggest an alternative.

### What to expect

The whole session can take up to **2 hours** (usually around 1 and a half) and is intended to be collaborative. We aren’t looking for a finished, polished product. We care about **how you structure problems, ask questions, and reason about solutions.**
